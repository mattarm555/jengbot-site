JengBot docs

Everything JengBot does, and how to set it up. Most settings are on the dashboard; commands work too.

Getting started

  • Add JengBot to your server. You need the Manage Server permission there.
  • Set it up with the commands below. The web dashboard (needed for role panels and ticket settings) opens to everyone soon.
  • Drag JengBot’s role above any role you want it to hand out (Server Settings → Roles). Discord only lets a bot give roles below its own.
  • Run /ping to check it’s online.

Welcome messages

Greet new members in a channel, give them a starter role, and optionally DM them. The dashboard shows a live preview. Placeholders: {user} {username} {server} {memberCount}.

/welcome setup
Turn welcome messages on and choose the channel.Needs Manage Server.
/welcome message
Change the welcome text.
/welcome role
Give new members a role, or stop giving one.
/welcome title
Change the embed title (Premium).
/welcome style
Embed color (Premium) and avatar.
/welcome dm
Also send new members a private message (Premium).
/welcome preview
See exactly what new members get. Only you see it.
/welcome show
Show the current settings.
/welcome disable
Turn welcome messages off. Settings are kept.

Moderation

Every action is saved as a numbered case, the member is told why (by DM, when their DMs are open), and the action can be posted to a private log channel. JengBot won’t act on the server owner, on itself, or on anyone whose highest role is at or above yours or JengBot’s.

/warn
Warn a member. It's recorded as a case.Needs Moderate Members.
/timeout
Stop a member from chatting for a while (up to 28 days).Needs Moderate Members.
/untimeout
End a timeout early.Needs Moderate Members.
/kick
Remove a member. They can rejoin with an invite.Needs Kick Members.
/ban
Ban someone, even if they already left. Can delete their recent messages.Needs Ban Members.
/unban
Lift a ban, by user ID.Needs Ban Members.
/cases
Recent cases, for everyone or one person.Needs Moderate Members.
/modlog
Choose the log channel, or stop logging.Needs Manage Server.

Role panels

A message with buttons or a dropdown that lets members pick their own roles: pronouns, games, ping roles, name colors. Make them on the dashboard under Role panels. A panel can allow several roles or just one at a time. JengBot refuses roles that would grant moderator powers.

Tickets

Members open a private channel that only they and your staff can see. Choose a category, staff roles, the first message and a log channel on the dashboard. When a ticket closes, the log channel gets a summary: who opened it, who closed it and why.

/ticket
Open a ticket with a subject.
/close
Close the ticket in this channel, with an optional reason.Needs the opener, staff, or Manage Channels.
/ticketpanel
Post an "Open a ticket" button in a channel.Needs Manage Server.

XP and levels

Members earn 15–25 XP per message, at most once a minute, and level up. Announce level-ups in the channel or a set channel, give reward roles at chosen levels, and skip channels where XP shouldn’t count. JengBot never reads what anyone writes; it only notices that a message was sent.

/rank
Your level and XP, or someone else's.
/leaderboard
The most active members.
/xp add | remove | reset
Change a member's XP.Needs Manage Server.

Newcomer insights

Who stayed? The dashboard’s Insights tab shows how the last 30 days of newcomers settled in: how many said hello, how long that took, how many were still around and still talking a week later, and how many left within a day. Use it to see whether your welcome, rules or onboarding are working.

JengBot only notes when someone joins, speaks and leaves, never what they write, and only counts members who join while tracking is on. Staff see totals, not individuals. Turn it off any time on the Insights tab; records are deleted after 180 days. Premium adds a week-by-week view of the last 12 weeks.

Weekly report (Premium): pick a staff channel on the Insights tab and every Monday at 09:00 UTC JengBot posts last week in one message: who joined, said hello and left within a day, how the week before’s newcomers held up, and how many tickets and moderation actions there were. It never pings anyone. Use Send a test report to see one right away.

Premium

$2.99 per server, per month. Every module above works on the free plan; Premium raises the limits:

  • Role panels: 3 → 25
  • XP level rewards: 3 → 25, plus custom XP amounts and cooldown
  • Ticket staff roles: 1 → 10, and up to 5 open tickets per member
  • A custom welcome title and color, and welcome DMs
  • Newcomer insights week by week, for the last 12 weeks
  • A weekly server report posted to a staff channel every Monday

If Premium ends, nothing is deleted. The extra settings pause and come back when Premium does. See your server’s plan with /premium or on the dashboard’s Premium tab.

Permissions

JengBot asks for exactly what its features use, never Administrator: View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Read Message History (posting, and letting ticket members share files), Manage Roles (welcome roles, role panels, level rewards), Manage Channels (tickets), and Kick, Ban and Timeout Members (moderation).

It uses the Server Members intent to see when someone joins. It does not use the Message Content intent.
